The Opportunity

We're looking for real opportunities

Do you have a business, confirmed order, contract, inventory opportunity, project or other revenue-generating opportunity that requires capital?

Submit your details and tell us exactly what you need the capital for, how the opportunity generates revenue, and what evidence you have.

AURUM Bridge is currently developing its funding and financing network, working toward connecting eligible opportunities with appropriate capital partners.

Focus

What kind of opportunities?

We evaluate opportunities that are specific and explainable — not vague requests for money.

01

Business Expansion

Capital requirements for clearly defined expansion opportunities.

02

Inventory & Trade

Opportunities involving the purchase, movement or sale of goods.

03

Confirmed Orders

Businesses with customers or orders that require working capital to fulfil.

04

Contracts & Projects

Revenue-generating projects requiring capital to execute.

05

Receivables

Businesses with legitimate receivables or expected business payments.

06

Other Opportunities

Other well-defined opportunities with a clear economic model.

Fit check

This may be for you if...

  • You already operate a business
  • You have a specific transaction or opportunity
  • You have customers or evidence of demand
  • You need capital for a clearly defined purpose
  • You can explain how the opportunity generates revenue
  • You are willing to provide supporting information if requested
Important notice

AURUM Bridge is currently developing its funding and financing network. Submission of an opportunity does not guarantee approval, funding, investment or financial returns.

Funding, where available, will be subject to eligibility, due diligence, applicable laws, regulatory requirements and the terms of the relevant financing partner.
